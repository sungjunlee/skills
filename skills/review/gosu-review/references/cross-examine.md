# gosu-review cross-examination

Read this when the panel conflicts. `SKILL.md` decides whether to run a round and which conflicts; this file is the brief and how to read the returns.

## The brief

Send each side the other's position. Both sides get the same shape, so neither is cast as the defendant.

```text
Another panelist reviewed the same target and reached a conflicting position.

Their claim: <what>
Their evidence: <evidence>
Their fix: <fix>

Your position was: <what> / <evidence> / <fix>

Go back to the artifact and check their position against it. You are not
defending your entry — you are finding out which position the artifact supports.

Return:
- position: hold | concede | refine
- why: what in the artifact decides it, cited the same way your findings were
- resolved fix: the action you would now take

Concede when they are right; that is the round working, not a loss. Holding
without new evidence from the artifact is not holding — if you have nothing
further to check, concede or say the question cannot be settled from the
artifact alone.
```

Resume the original panelist when the host supports it. They have already read the target, so the round is cheap and their new evidence is continuous with their old. Otherwise dispatch a fresh subagent carrying the persona, both positions, and the original context.

## Reading the result

- **One concedes** — the tension is resolved. Say who moved and on what evidence. A concession backed by a citation is the strongest output this skill produces, because two independent readings converged on the artifact.
- **One refines** — usually the real answer. The fix that survives is narrower than either opening position; report the narrowed version, not a merge of both.
- **Both hold with new evidence** — the disagreement is real. Report it unresolved and name what would settle it. A fabricated middle is worse than an honest open question.
- **Both hold without new evidence** — the round failed, not the artifact. Say so plainly and fall back to reporting both positions as the panel left them.
